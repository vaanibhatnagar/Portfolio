Case Study: Building Sustainable Community Partnerships at Dearborn STEM Academy
Background
As part of my Affordable Design and Entrepreneurship (ADE) class, our Community Development Team collaborated closely with Dearborn STEM Academy (DSA), a community-focused school located in Boston's Roxbury neighborhood. Under the guidance of advisors Dr. Kofi Taha and Dr. Amon Milner, our goal was to foster sustainable, long-term growth through empowering educational experiences.
The Challenge
DSA's mission emphasizes not only academic success but also contributions to community health and vibrancy—a unique perspective rooted in Roxbury’s history of community organizing against systemic inequities. Our challenge was to support DSA in enhancing student engagement in STEM, creating inclusive spaces, and encouraging students to envision expansive opportunities for their futures.
Research & Insights
Through active classroom involvement, mentorship, and deep conversations with teachers and administrators, several insights guided our initiatives:
Building trust through consistent, genuine engagement was crucial for meaningful partnerships.
Students thrived in environments offering creative freedom, practical challenges, and mentorship opportunities.
Young women at DSA were underrepresented in STEM fields and needed targeted encouragement and support.
Early exposure to college environments significantly impacted students' perception of higher education opportunities.
Our Solution
Based on these insights, we implemented four primary initiatives during our partnership:
In-Class STEM Engagement: Collaborated with teachers to design interactive engineering activities, including marble roller coaster competitions and a comprehensive Rube Goldberg machine project, fostering creativity, curiosity, and teamwork.
After-School Program: Established an after-school space focused on student-driven projects, notably repairing and utilizing 3D printers. This approach empowered students by promoting ownership, confidence, and skill development.
Girls in STEM Initiative: Partnered with teachers to create targeted discussions for young women, aimed at fostering interest and confidence in engineering and computer science pathways.
College Exploration: Organized an immersive field trip to Olin College, providing practical exposure to college life and interactive STEM activities, significantly enhancing student enthusiasm and aspirations for higher education.
Impact
Our initiatives were met with enthusiasm and meaningful engagement from students and faculty:
Students demonstrated increased confidence and creativity through practical STEM experiences.
Teachers reported stronger student interest and participation, particularly from previously underrepresented groups.
The college field trip inspired students, making the possibility of college more tangible and attainable.
Reflections & Growth
Working with DSA taught our team the power and necessity of building sustained, trust-based relationships. Questions from teachers, such as, "Can you stay connected with these students long-term?" emphasized that our most significant contributions extended beyond immediate activities, highlighting the value of deep interpersonal connections.
Looking Ahead
Moving forward, we aim to deepen relationships between Olin and DSA communities beyond structured programs, fostering lasting friendships, mentorships, and peer connections. These ongoing relationships will support continued personal growth and community empowerment for students at DSA.
